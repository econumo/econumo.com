Manage Money.
Together.

Econumo [eh-koh-NOO-mo] is a beautiful application for managing personal and family finances, supporting multiple currencies, joint accounts, and budgets, offering cloud-hosted or self-hosted options.

Key Features

  • Shared budgets & accounts. Plan and spend against one budget with your partner or family, with joint accounts and notes on any number.

  • Automatic transactions. Apple Pay purchases arrive through Apple Wallet and bank transactions sync through SimpleFIN — or add them by hand in seconds.

  • Savings and debt tracking. Watch savings grow toward your goals and debts shrink as you pay them down.

  • Multi-currency. Keep accounts in any currency, in an interface translated into 11 languages.

  • AI assistant and API. Connect Claude or any other MCP-compatible assistant to check budgets and log transactions from a chat, or build your own integrations on the API.
