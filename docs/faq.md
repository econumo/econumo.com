# Frequently Asked Questions

## Can Econumo import transactions from my bank automatically?

Yes, now it can. Econumo was built around entering transactions by hand: it's
quick, it keeps you aware of every expense, and it works with any bank in any
country. Manual entry is still at the heart of the app, but you no longer have
to type everything in:

- **[Apple Wallet](/docs/user-guide/apple-wallet/)** — an iPhone Shortcut sends
  every Apple Pay purchase you make with a tap to Econumo.
- **[SimpleFIN](/docs/user-guide/simplefin/)** — SimpleFIN Bridge, a paid
  third-party service, connects to your bank (mostly in the US and Canada), and
  Econumo pulls your transactions from it each time you sync.

[Import rules](/docs/user-guide/import-rules/) then classify imported
transactions for you. Both integrations are optional; you can mix them with
manual entry, and use [CSV import](/docs/user-guide/csv-import-export/#importing-transactions-from-csv)
for anything else.

If you have development skills and want to connect another source, you are
welcome to do so through Econumo's [API](/docs/api/).
