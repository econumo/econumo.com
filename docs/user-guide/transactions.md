# Transactions

This section provides an overview of Transactions in Econumo.

Transactions form the core of Econumo, as all budgeting is based on the
transactions you enter. Open any account to see its transaction list, search
it, and add new records. To find a transaction across all your accounts at
once, use [Search](/docs/user-guide/search/) (**Ctrl+K** / **⌘K**).

On an account page, drag the marker along the timeline above the list to
scroll through its history — the date shows as you drag — or click the marker
to jump straight to a month and year.

## Adding transactions

Press **Add transaction** on the account page (on mobile it is the button at
the bottom of the screen) to open the transaction dialog.

**Transaction date**

In the top-right corner of the dialog, you can set the transaction date (the
date you spent or earned the money). Clicking the date opens a calendar, and
the arrow next to it steps one day back. Note that transaction time is not
currently supported.

**Transaction types**

You can add one of the following transaction types:

- **Expense** (default option)
- **Transfer**
- **Income**

Each type has a different set of fields to complete.

---

### Adding expenses

To add an expense, pick the account, enter the amount, and choose a category —
that is all a record needs. Notes, a recipient, and tags are optional.

</ScreenshotSet>

**Amount**

Enter the amount spent in the account's currency. The `+`, `−`, `×`, `÷`, and
`=` buttons under the amount field (also available as keyboard keys on
desktop) let you calculate the total right in the dialog — for example, add up
several receipt lines and press `=`.

**Categories**

Categories are optional — you can save an expense without picking one. Click
the category field to search your categories or create a new one: type a name
that does not exist yet and press "Enter" (or "Search"/"Submit" on your mobile
keyboard). The name must be 3–64 characters long.

On desktop and tablet, the category field opens an inline dropdown under
itself: the first match is highlighted as you type, the arrow keys move the
highlight, and Enter picks it. On a phone it opens as a sheet instead, with
the category list above a search field pinned right above the keyboard, so
typing never pushes the list or the keyboard around.

**Note**
Leave a transaction uncategorized and it still counts toward your budget: it
shows up there as a read-only **Uncategorized** element instead of being left
out of the totals. See [Budgeting](/docs/user-guide/budgets/#budgeting).

</ScreenshotSet>

**Recipients**

You can specify a recipient for each transaction — the name of a person or a
company where you spent the money. Like categories, recipients can be created
right from the dialog, and the field behaves the same way: an inline dropdown
on desktop and tablet, a sheet on a phone.

**Note**
Every searchable picker in the app opens as a phone sheet the same way —
accounts, the currency field in the account, budget, envelope and profile
forms, a budget's **Accounts** and **Savings accounts** lists, and **Merge
into…**.

**Tags**

Tags group spending across categories. The **Tags** row lists both kinds side
by side, and they behave differently when you tap them:

- **Budget tags**, marked with `#`, are for a project or an event — in the
  screenshots, the family tags its Italy vacation expenses with `Italy 2026`.
  A transaction takes only one, so picking a second replaces the first.
- **Reporting tags** track who or what the money went on — a kid, a pet, a
  second home. Toggle as many as apply.

Press the `+` button to create a new tag without leaving the dialog (3–64
characters); see
[Budget tags and reporting tags](/docs/user-guide/classifications/#budget-tags-and-reporting-tags)
for the difference in full.

**Notes**

The notes field allows you to add any comments related to the transaction.

### Adding transfers

Transfers move money between your accounts without affecting the budget:

**Note**
**Transfer** is greyed out in the transaction dialog unless you have at least
two visible accounts you can add transactions to — accounts in a hidden folder
don't count. Editing an existing transfer still works.

**From/To**

Select the account **from** which you want to move money **to** another
account. The swap button next to the account fields switches the direction.

**Amount**

Enter the transfer amount. The same in-dialog calculator (`+`, `−`, `×`, `÷`,
`=`) is available. If the two accounts use different currencies, a second field
appears so you can record the exact amount received in the target currency:

### Adding income

Recording an income works just like an expense: pick the account, enter the
amount, and choose an income category (income categories are separate from
expense categories). You can also set a **sender** — the person or company you
received the money from.

## Reordering transactions

Put a day's transactions in the same order as your bank statement. On
desktop, hover a row and drag it by the grip that appears in place of its
icon. On a phone or tablet, press and hold a row, then drag it.

Dropping a row into another day moves it to that day and keeps its time. On a
transfer, each account keeps its own order, so reordering one side never
moves the other.

## Editing a transaction

On desktop and tablet, open the three-dots menu on a transaction row and choose
**Edit**. On mobile, tap the transaction to see its details, then press
**Edit**.

You can update any transaction in your own accounts, and in shared accounts
where you have at least **Manage transactions** access.

## Deleting a transaction

Choose **Delete** in the same menu (or in the details sheet on mobile) and
confirm. You can delete any transaction except transactions in view-only
accounts.
