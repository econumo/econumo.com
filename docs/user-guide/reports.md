# Reports

Reports answer bigger questions than a single month's budget. Open them with
the chart icon at the bottom of the sidebar, next to the sync button (on
mobile, at the bottom of the home screen).

The first screen is a list of reports to choose from. The picker in its
top-right corner sets the **scope**: **My accounts**, or one of your budgets —
a shared budget covers everyone taking part in it. Each report card previews
its answer; **Net worth** shows today's total and how much it changed this
year.

## Net worth

**Net worth** answers *"Am I getting richer or poorer?"*. It draws one line
for the total of your accounts over time, with the change for the period
underneath. Pick the period with **1y**, **3y** or **All** — **All** starts
at your first transaction.

The table under the chart shows where the change came from, with **Start**,
**Now** and **Change** columns:

- For **My accounts**, one row per account folder; open a folder to see its
  accounts.
- For a budget, one row per person taking part in it. Open a row to see that
  person's savings accounts by name, plus one **Everyday accounts** total for
  the rest. Another person's accounts that aren't shared with you are never
  listed one by one — only their total.

Select an account row to list its transactions.

To look at a shorter stretch, **drag across the chart**. The figures follow
your selection, which replaces the period buttons, and a **Transactions**
button lists what happened in that range.

**Note**
Deleted accounts are left out of the report completely, including their
history.

## Report tabs

Reports open in tabs at the top of the page, so you can keep several open side
by side — for example, net worth for your own accounts in one tab and for the
family budget in another. **+** opens a new tab with the report list. A tab's
menu can rename, duplicate or close it. Tabs are remembered on the device you
use.
