# Import data

Econumo was built around entering transactions by hand, and that still works
everywhere, with any bank. When you'd rather not type everything in, there are
three ways to bring transactions in, and you can mix them with manual entry
freely. All of them live in the **Data** group of **Settings**.

| | What it brings in | Good for |
|---|---|---|
| [CSV Import & Export](/docs/user-guide/csv-import-export/) | A file exported from your bank or another app | Your history, or a bank with no other connection; also exports your data |
| [Apple Wallet](/docs/user-guide/apple-wallet/) | Each Apple Pay purchase you tap to pay, as it happens | Card spending on an iPhone |
| [SimpleFIN](/docs/user-guide/simplefin/) | Your bank's transactions, each time you sync | Banks in the US and Canada, through the paid SimpleFIN Bridge service |

Transactions that arrive from Apple Wallet or SimpleFIN come with the bank's
text rather than your categories. [Import rules](/docs/user-guide/import-rules/)
classify them for you — or skip the ones you don't want — and with AI available
on your server, Econumo suggests rules as new imports come in.

Anything an import can't place on its own waits for you in the
[Inbox](/docs/user-guide/inbox/#imported-transactions).
