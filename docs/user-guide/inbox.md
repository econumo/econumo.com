# Inbox

The Inbox collects everything that is waiting on you in one place. Open it with
the tray icon at the top of the sidebar (on mobile, at the top of the home
screen); its badge shows how many things are waiting.

## Scheduled transactions

[Recurring transactions](/docs/user-guide/recurring-transactions/) that are
due today or overdue come first, across all your accounts. Each row shows the
schedule's category, interval, note, payee, amount and account, and when it
was due — *"Overdue · Nov 12"*.

Select a row to open its **Transaction details**, then:

- **POST** — records the payment as a real transaction, and the schedule
  moves on to its next date.
- **Skip** — the step-over icon beside it — passes on this occurrence without
  recording anything.

Either way the schedule moves on to its next date.

## Imported transactions

If you connect [Apple Wallet](/docs/user-guide/apple-wallet/) or
[SimpleFIN](/docs/user-guide/simplefin/), the Inbox also collects what the
import couldn't finish on its own:

- **Sync problems** — a SimpleFIN sync that failed or finished with errors,
  with the time of the last attempt.
- **Failed imports** and **To review** — transactions that couldn't go
  straight into an account, with the reason: a card or bank account that isn't
  mapped yet, an account you no longer have access to, or a currency without an
  exchange rate. **Import** one into an account, **Skip** it (skipped ones stay
  under **Skipped** and can be restored), **Retry** it, or **Discard** it.
- **Suggested rules** — [AI rule suggestions](/docs/user-guide/import-rules/#ai-rule-suggestions)
  for imported transactions that no rule classified.

## Changes that weren't saved

Econumo keeps working when you lose your connection: transactions you add,
edit or delete, and plan changes, wait on the device and are sent when you are
back online. If the server doesn't accept one of them, it lands under **Not
saved**, showing what the change was. **Try again** sends it once more;
**Discard** drops it.

## Sharing invites

When someone shares an [account](/docs/user-guide/accounts/#shared-access) or
a [budget](/docs/user-guide/budgets/#shared-access) with you, the invite waits
in the **Sharing** group at the bottom of the Inbox, saying who invited you
and to what. **Accept** it — for an account, you first choose the folder to
file it under — or **Decline** it. See
[Shared Access](/docs/user-guide/shared-access/#accept-or-decline-a-sharing-request)
for the details.
