# Inbox

The Inbox collects everything that is waiting on you in one place. Open it with
the tray icon at the top of the sidebar (on mobile, at the top of the home
screen); its badge shows how many things are waiting.

## Scheduled transactions

[Recurring transactions](/docs/user-guide/recurring-transactions/) that are
due today or overdue come first, across all your accounts. Each row shows the
schedule's category, interval, note, payee, amount and account, and when it
was due — *"Overdue · Aug 28"*.

Select a row to open its **Transaction details**, then:

- **POST** — records the payment as a real transaction, and the schedule
  moves on to its next date.
- **Skip** — the step-over icon beside it — passes on this occurrence without
  recording anything.

Either way the schedule moves on to its next date.

## Sharing invites

When someone shares an [account](/docs/user-guide/accounts/#shared-access) or
a [budget](/docs/user-guide/budgets/#shared-access) with you, the invite waits
in the **Sharing** group at the bottom of the Inbox, saying who invited you
and to what. **Accept** it — for an account, you first choose the folder to
file it under — or **Decline** it. See
[Shared Access](/docs/user-guide/shared-access/#accept-or-decline-a-sharing-request)
for the details.
