# Debts

A debt tracks a loan — a mortgage, a car loan, a student loan — inside your
[budget](/docs/user-guide/budgets/), without opening an account for it. Your
net worth and account totals stay clean, and the budget still shows how the
loan is going: how many payments are left, how much of each payment is
interest, and when the loan will be paid off.

A debt sits on an expense category (or an envelope). The expenses you already
record in that category are its payments, so nothing changes in how you enter
or import transactions. Each payment is split bank-style into **interest** and
**principal**, and Econumo works out the rate your lender is charging from the
loan's terms — you never have to type it in.

## Tracking a loan as a debt

Give the loan its own expense category first (for example _Car Loan_) and
record its payments there, from an account that belongs to the budget. Then,
on the budget page:

1. Open the category row's **⋮** menu and choose **Track as debt**. On a
   tablet or a phone, press **Configure → Edit structure** first — the rows
   get their **⋮** menus there.
2. Enter the regular **Payment** — Econumo suggests the amount planned for the
   row — and how often it **Repeats** (every week, every 2 weeks, every month,
   every quarter or every year).
3. Press **Add**.

The debt starts collecting its payments straight away. To see the balance, the
payments left and the payoff date, finish setting it up: open
[Debt details](/docs/user-guide/debts/#debt-details), go to **Settings**, and
enter the **Amount borrowed**, the **Start date** (when the loan began) and the
**Last payment date** (when the loan ends).

**Note**
Only expenses on the budget's own accounts count as payments. If you pay the
loan from an account that isn't part of the budget, add that account in
[Budget settings](/docs/user-guide/budgets/#update-budget-details) first.

## Debt details

Open the debt row's **⋮** menu and choose **Debt → Debt details**. On a phone,
tap the row and then **Debt details** in its sheet. The details open in a
dialog with four tabs.

### Overview

The top shows how many payments are left, with a bar for the payments made so
far (*"6 of 45 paid"*). Under it, **Loan** lists the balance today and the rate
Econumo worked out, the amount borrowed and when, the payment and how often it
repeats, the next and the last payment, and how much you have paid — and still
plan to pay — on top of the schedule. **Payments** lists every payment with its
interest, principal and the balance after it; **Show future payments** adds
the schedule ahead.

### Balance

**Balance today** compares Econumo's figure with your bank statement. When your
lender sends a statement, press **Add a statement** and enter the balance it
shows; Econumo uses it from that date on. **Rate today** shows the rate and the
payment in force. If your lender changes the rate, press **Add a rate change**.

### Settings

The loan's terms: **Amount borrowed**, **Start date**, **Last payment date**,
**Payment** and **Repeats**. Correct them here if you entered something wrong;
Econumo splits every payment again with the new terms.

### Paying off or deleting a debt

The **Danger zone** tab has two buttons:

- **Mark as paid off** sets what is owed to zero and stops counting down the
  payments — use it when you settle the last small amount.
- **Delete** removes the debt and its history. Your transactions are not
  changed.

Both are also in the row's **⋮** menu under **Debt**.

## Debts in your budget and plan

A debt stays in its row. Under the name it shows the payments left and the
payoff date (*"40 payments left · Paid off by Feb 2030"*), and its **Budget**
and **Spent** columns work like any other row.

Plan the debt as you would any category, in the budget or the
[Plan view](/docs/user-guide/plan/). Planning more than the scheduled payment
counts as an extra payment: the payoff date moves earlier, and the overview
shows it under **Planned extra**.

---

## Debts in a shared budget

Everyone with access to the budget sees each debt by name, with its payments
and balance — the **Budget settings** dialog says so under the accounts. People
who can manage the budget can also open and change its details; with view-only
access, you can look but not edit.
