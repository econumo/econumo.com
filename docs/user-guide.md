# User Guide

This guide covers the essentials of using Econumo — from your first sign-in to
family budgeting with shared accounts.

Econumo works the same way in a desktop browser, on a tablet, and on a phone.
Throughout this guide, every screen is shown on all three form factors: the
desktop layout with the sidebar, the compact tablet layout, and the mobile
layout, where lists open full-screen and actions live in bottom sheets.

## Sign Up

Switch to the **Sign up** tab and provide your name (or nickname), email, and
password (entered twice to confirm). The form is the same whether you use
Econumo at [app.econumo.com](https://app.econumo.com/register) or Econumo
Self-Hosted.

<ScreenshotSet>

</ScreenshotSet>

**Econumo at app.econumo.com**
A new account starts with a 45-day free trial, and no payment card is required
to sign up. To keep using Econumo after the trial, make a one-time payment.
See [Econumo Editions](/docs/edition/) for details.

**Econumo Self-Hosted**
Administrators can disable open registration with the
`ECONUMO_ALLOW_REGISTRATION` environment variable and create users from the
[CLI](/docs/self-hosting/cli-commands/) instead.

## Sign In

To sign in, enter the email and password you provided during sign-up. If your
Econumo instance enables single sign-on, an **or continue with** divider below
the form offers **Google**, **Apple**, or a custom OpenID Connect provider,
alongside the password form.

<ScreenshotSet>

</ScreenshotSet>

**Note**
Signing in through a provider for the first time creates a new, passwordless
account if your server allows registration. You can add a password afterwards
from **Settings → Personal settings → Change password**, or link the provider to an
account you already have from
[Sign-in methods](/docs/user-guide/user-profile/#sign-in-methods).

**Econumo Self-Hosted**
Administrators can turn password sign-in off entirely with the
`ECONUMO_PASSWORD_LOGIN` environment variable. On such an instance the sign-in
and sign-up screens show only the provider buttons — no email/password
fields, no **or continue with** divider, and no **Forgot password?** link —
and **Settings → Personal settings → Security** no longer offers **Change password** or
**Set a password**.

## Password Recovery

Click **Forgot password?** on the sign-in screen, enter your email, and press
**Send code**.

<ScreenshotSet>

</ScreenshotSet>

If an account exists with the provided email, you will receive a security code.
Enter the code, choose a new password, and press **Reset password**:

<ScreenshotSet>

</ScreenshotSet>

**Note**
Resetting your password is a deliberate account reclaim, not just a password
change: it signs you out everywhere, revokes API tokens, and unlinks any
sign-in provider that vouches for a different email address than your own.
Your shared budgets and connections are left alone.

## Getting started

After your first sign-in, Econumo walks you through setup in a few short
steps. The bar at the top shows where you are (*"Getting started · 2 of 5"*);
tap a step to jump to it.

1. **Import** *(optional)* — **Bring in your history**: choose a CSV export
   from your bank or another app, and Econumo creates accounts, categories and
   transactions in one go (see [CSV Import](/docs/user-guide/csv-import-export/#importing-transactions-from-csv)).
   No file? Press **I don't have one**.
2. **Categories** — **What do you spend on?** Econumo suggests a starting list
   of expense and income categories. Remove what you don't need, rename them,
   change their icons, or add your own.
3. **Accounts** — **Where's your money right now?** Add your accounts with
   their current balances and currencies; no history is needed.
4. **Budget & plan** — **Plan this month**: enter rough planned amounts for
   your income and expenses. You can adjust them later.
5. **Connect** — **Connect your accounts** so transactions come in on their
   own: set up [Apple Wallet](/docs/user-guide/apple-wallet/) or
   [SimpleFIN](/docs/user-guide/simplefin/), or copy the MCP address for an AI
   assistant such as Claude or ChatGPT.

When your browser can install Econumo, an extra step offers to add it to your
home screen (see
[Installing Econumo as an app](/docs/user-guide/#installing-econumo-as-an-app)).

<ScreenshotSet>

</ScreenshotSet>

Press **Complete setup** when you are done — you can do it from any step.
Everything the wizard sets up stays available in **Settings** afterwards.

## Finding your way around

Your accounts, grouped into folders, fill the sidebar on desktop and tablet and
the home screen on mobile; **Budget & Plan** above them opens your
[budget](/docs/user-guide/budgets/) and its
[Plan view](/docs/user-guide/plan/). Next to the Econumo logo at the top sit
two buttons: [Search](/docs/user-guide/search/) (also on **Ctrl+K** / **⌘K**)
and the [Inbox](/docs/user-guide/inbox/), whose badge counts what is waiting on
you. At the bottom, next to **Settings**, the chart icon opens
[Reports](/docs/user-guide/reports/) and the sync button refreshes your data
(and syncs [SimpleFIN](/docs/user-guide/simplefin/), if you use it).

Everything that is not day-to-day spending lives in **Settings**, in these
groups:

- **Billing** — **Manage subscription**, on econumo.com (not on self-hosted
  servers).
- **Finances** — [Accounts](/docs/user-guide/accounts/),
  [Shared access](/docs/user-guide/shared-access/), **Budgets & Plans**, and
  [Recurring transactions](/docs/user-guide/recurring-transactions/).
- **Classification** — [Categories, Tags, Payees](/docs/user-guide/classifications/)
  and [Currencies](/docs/user-guide/currencies/).
- **Data** — the ways to [import data](/docs/user-guide/import-data/):
  **Import & export** for
  [CSV import and export](/docs/user-guide/csv-import-export/),
  [Apple Wallet](/docs/user-guide/apple-wallet/),
  [SimpleFIN](/docs/user-guide/simplefin/),
  [Import rules](/docs/user-guide/import-rules/), and **Clear cache and
  reload**, which downloads all your data again.
- **Feedback** — **Send feedback**: tell us what you like, ask for a feature,
  or report a bug.

On desktop and tablet, open Settings from the bottom of the sidebar; on mobile,
from the bottom of the home screen. The card with your name at the top opens
your [Personal settings](/docs/user-guide/user-profile/), **Log out** sits
right under it, and the **API** link at the very bottom opens the
[API reference](/docs/api/) for your instance. Econumo can also install
itself as an app on your phone or computer — Settings offers an **Install the
app** row, and a banner may invite you to do the same.

**Note**
Transactions are kept on your device so accounts you've already seen load
instantly, and only accounts that changed are downloaded again. After an
upgrade to a new version (such as v1.8), a card at the bottom of the sidebar
links to its release notes; dismissing it on one device dismisses it
everywhere.

## Installing Econumo as an app

Econumo can live on your home screen or in your Dock and open full-screen, like
any other app. When your browser supports it, **Settings** shows **Install the
app**; it opens your browser's install prompt or step-by-step instructions —
on an iPhone or iPad, for example, **Share → Add to Home Screen**.

Once installed, Econumo starts even without a connection.

---
## Working offline

Econumo keeps your recent data on the device, so you can keep going when the
connection drops: the sync button turns into a no-connection icon, and you can
still add, edit and delete transactions and change your budget plans. The
changes wait on the device and are sent when you are back online. The default
budget's previous, current and next months are kept for offline use; anything
that isn't stored on the device shows **Not available offline**.

If the server doesn't accept a change made offline, you'll find it in the
[Inbox](/docs/user-guide/inbox/#changes-that-werent-saved).
