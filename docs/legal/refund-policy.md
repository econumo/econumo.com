# Refund Policy

This Refund Policy applies to purchases of **Econumo**, the hosted service at
[app.econumo.com](https://app.econumo.com/). **Econumo Self-Hosted** is free and open source, so no refunds apply to it. Please read this policy together with our [Terms of Service](/docs/legal/terms-of-service/).

## 1. Purchases and Merchant of Record

Purchases of Econumo are processed by [Paddle](https://www.paddle.com/), which acts as the Merchant of Record
for our orders. This means Paddle handles billing, payment processing, applicable taxes, and payment-related
customer inquiries. Your order confirmation and receipt are issued by Paddle, and the charge on your statement will
appear under Paddle's name.

## 2. Try Before You Buy

We recommend evaluating Econumo before purchasing. Every new account comes with a **45-day free trial**, and no
payment card is required to start it. You can sign up at [app.econumo.com](https://app.econumo.com/register). You
can also run **Econumo Self-Hosted** for free to see whether the Software meets your needs.

## 3. Refund Window

If Econumo does not work out for you, you can request a **full refund within 14 days of purchase**, no questions
asked. To request a refund, either:

- Contact Paddle directly at [paddle.net](https://paddle.net/), using the email address you used at checkout or the
  order details from your receipt; or
- Email us at [econumo@gmail.com](mailto:econumo@gmail.com) with your order number, and we will arrange the refund
  with Paddle on your behalf.

## 4. After the Refund Window

After the 14-day window has passed, refunds are issued at our discretion. We will consider a refund in exceptional
cases, for example when a technical issue caused by us prevents you from using Econumo and we have been unable
to resolve it. Please contact us at [econumo@gmail.com](mailto:econumo@gmail.com) and describe the problem.

## 5. How Refunds Are Paid

Refunds are issued to the original payment method used for the purchase. Depending on your bank or card issuer, it
may take several business days for the refund to appear on your statement. Once a refund is issued, access to
Econumo for the refunded purchase is revoked.

## 6. Your Statutory Rights

Nothing in this policy limits any rights you have under applicable consumer protection law. In particular, if you are
a consumer in the European Union, the United Kingdom, or another jurisdiction with statutory withdrawal or
cancellation rights, those rights are not affected by this policy.

## 7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Refund Policy from time to time. We will post changes on this page with a new effective date.
Changes do not affect purchases made before they take effect.

## 8. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Refund Policy, please reach out: econumo@gmail.com
