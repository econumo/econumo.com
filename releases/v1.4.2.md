# v1.4.2

Published: September 16, 2026
URL: https://econumo.com/releases/v1.4.2/

The **v1.4.2** release changes how a due or overdue recurring transaction shows
up and gets posted, so it reads as something still waiting on you rather than
something that already happened on some old date. No breaking changes, no
`.env` changes, no manual steps to upgrade.

## Due recurring transactions now lead Today

A recurring template that's due today, or overdue, used to sit in your account's
transaction list at its own scheduled date — dimmed grey, buried under whatever
real transactions came after it, reading as if it had already happened. Now it
pins to the top of the **Today** group instead, ahead of today's real
transactions, so it reads as still waiting on you.

To see it in your own account:

1. Open any account with recurring transactions on it.
2. Look at the **Today** group at the top of the list. Any template that's due
   today or overdue shows there first, each marked **Not posted** — ahead of
   today's real transactions, not sorted in with them.
3. A template that's still ahead of schedule is untouched — it stays grouped at
   its own future date further down the list, not pulled up to Today.

Posting one now dates the transaction correctly, too:

1. Open **Settings → Recurring**.
2. Open the **⋮** menu on a due or overdue row and tap **Post**.
3. The review dialog opens with its date chip pre-filled to **today**, not the
   missed scheduled date — still editable if you want a different day. A
   template that isn't overdue keeps pre-filling its own scheduled date, as
   before.

**Note**
This is a frontend-only change to how the list groups and sorts, and to what
date a posted transaction gets — the schedule itself, and when the next payment
comes due, work exactly as before.

## More in this release

- Fixed: stored exchange rates weren't applied to budgets on SQLite.
- Fixed: SQLite datetimes are now stored in the frozen layout, and budget
  balances chain correctly.
- Dependency updates across the Go and frontend stacks.
