# v1.5.1

Published: September 22, 2026
URL: https://econumo.com/releases/v1.5.1/

The **v1.5.1** release is a small follow-up to [v1.5.0](/releases/v1.5.0/): it fixes a display bug on iPhones where signing in with Apple could leave a white band at the bottom of the app.

**Self-hosting Econumo?** Upgrade notes are in [For self-hosters](#for-self-hosters) at the end of this page.

## Changelog

- Fixed a white band that could appear below the app on iPhone home-screen installs after signing in with Apple. It stayed through reloads and rotations until you relaunched the app. The app and its full-screen pages (sign-in, the 404 page and others) now fit the screen correctly in every state.

## For self-hosters

There are no breaking changes and no database migrations. Upgrading is a straight image bump.
